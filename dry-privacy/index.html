Dry Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 April 2026

Dry is a personal dry-day tracking app created by Tristan Thomas. This policy explains what information the app processes and how it is used.

Information you provide in the app

Dry stores the days you mark as Dry or Drank, your weekly target, your calendar settings, and your display theme.

How your data is used

Dry uses your data to:

  • Show your calendar and weekly progress.
  • Calculate stats such as streaks, averages, and monthly totals.
  • Keep your widget up to date.
  • Sync your tracking data across your own devices when iCloud is available.

Storage and syncing

Your tracking data is stored locally on your device. If iCloud is available and enabled for Dry, your data may also be stored in your private iCloud account so it can sync across your own devices.

If iCloud is not available, Dry falls back to local on-device storage.

Accounts, analytics, and advertising

Dry does not require you to create an account. Dry does not include third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

Data sharing

Dry does not sell your personal data. Data may be processed by Apple platform services, such as iCloud, only as needed to provide app storage and syncing features.

Deleting your data

You can remove local app data by deleting Dry from your device. iCloud-synced data can be managed through your Apple ID and iCloud storage settings.

Contact

If you have privacy questions about Dry, contact t@tristanthomas.me.
